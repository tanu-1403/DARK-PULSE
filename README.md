# 🕶️ **DARK PULSE**
### *“Illuminating unseen patterns in a world shadowed by fear.”*

---

## 🌌 Inspiration

In **November 2008**, my family and I were in **Mumbai** during the tragic **Taj Hotel terrorist attacks**.  
My father, who was serving in the **Indian Navy**, was directly involved in the tense aftermath that followed.  
Experiencing those moments of uncertainty and resilience left a **deep and lasting impact** on me.

Coming from a **military family**, I grew up with an awareness of the cost of war, the weight of duty, and the silent aftermath of violence.  
**Dark Pulse** is born from that experience — a fusion of *personal memory and analytical pursuit*.  
It’s my attempt to use data as a medium to **understand**, **visualize**, and **learn** from the shadows that terrorism casts on humanity.

> “For me, this project is not just about data — it’s about empathy, remembrance, and the pursuit of peace through understanding.”

---

<details>
<summary>🧠 <b>What I Learned</b></summary>

Working on this project taught me how **data science** can transform human experiences into patterns that inform and educate.

- 🧩 **Data Preprocessing:** Cleaned and structured a dataset of 90k+ terror incidents from the Global Terrorism Database (GTD).   
- 🔢 **Statistical Insight:**  
  \[
  \text{Mean Casualties} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{casualties}_i}{n}
  \]  
  \[
  \text{Attack Frequency Index} = \frac{\text{Total Attacks}}{\text{Number of Years}}
  \] 
- 📈 **Visualization:** Built an interactive **Streamlit dashboard** using **Plotly** to visualize trends and connections.
</details>

---

<details>
<summary>🏗️ <b>How I Built It</b></summary>

### **Stage 1 — Data Cleaning & Exploration**
- Dataset: [Global Terrorism Database (GTD)](https://www.start.umd.edu/gtd/)  
- Tools: `pandas`, `numpy`  
- Handled missing entries, standardized categorical columns, and recoded attributes for consistency.

### **Stage 2 — Descriptive Analysis**
- Calculated:
  - **Total Attacks per Year**
  - **Casualty Distributions**
  - **Top Active Groups & Deadliest Regions**

### **Stage 3 — Visualization & Dashboard**
- Tools: `plotly.express`, `streamlit`  
- Visual Modules:
  - 🌍 *Geo Heatmap of global attacks*
  - 📆 *Temporal trend plots*
  - 🕸️ *Network graphs* linking regions, groups, and attack types  
</details>

---

<details>
<summary>⚙️ <b>Challenges I Faced</b></summary>

- 🧹 **Data Quality:** Missing or misclassified entries, especially in location and casualty columns.  
- 💻 **Performance:** Streamlit rendering delays due to dataset size (~150MB).  
- 🎯 **Feature Selection:** Choosing variables that best represent behavioral tendencies.  
- ⚖️ **Ethical Responsibility:** Balancing analytical accuracy with empathy and sensitivity to real-world loss.
</details>
